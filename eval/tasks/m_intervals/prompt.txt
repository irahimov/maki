Create intervals.py with merge_intervals(intervals: list[tuple[int, int]]) -> list[tuple[int, int]]: merges overlapping or touching intervals (for example (1, 3) and (3, 5) become (1, 5)) and returns them sorted by start. The input list must not be modified and may be unsorted. An interval with start > end raises ValueError. Also create schedule.py with free_slots(busy: list[tuple[int, int]], day_start: int, day_end: int) -> list[tuple[int, int]]: returns the free gaps inside the day that are not covered by any busy interval (busy intervals may overlap each other and may extend outside the day; use merge_intervals from intervals.py). Zero-length gaps are not returned and day_start >= day_end raises ValueError. Include tests.
